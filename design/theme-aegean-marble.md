# Aegean Marble

A custom theme made with the theme-factory skill. It is bright Greek in white, blue and gold: whitewashed walls and marble, the lapis blue of the Aegean, and the gold of laurel wreaths and temple offerings. It replaces the earlier Attic Kiln theme (terracotta and black glaze), which the generator in `design/kiln-viewer.html` can still make with its Palette setting.

## Colour Palette

- **Marble**: `#fbf8f1`. Card bodies, panels, the table rim and the Agora card back.
- **Lapis Blue**: `#1f4e9c`. Figures on Oracle cards, name bands, the table field and the Oracle card back.
- **Deep Navy**: `#16386f`. Text and outlines.
- **Gold**: `#d4a62a`. Card edges, the key pattern, points medallions and the glow on things you can play.
- **Pale Gold**: `#f2d27a`. Highlights, light parts of drawings and the turn banner.
- **Sky**: `#e8f3fc`. The light of the game room and the backs of panels.

## Typography

- **Headers**: Marcellus SC for card names and labels, and Cinzel for the interface.
- **Body text**: Alegreya Sans.

## Best used for

Every screen, card, card back and the table of Rivals of Olympus.
