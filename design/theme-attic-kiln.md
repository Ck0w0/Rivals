# Attic Kiln

A custom theme made with the theme-factory skill. Its colours are the materials of an Athenian painted vase. Every colour here could come out of a kiln in Athens: fired clay, the black glaze, and the two "added" colours painters laid over it.

## Colour Palette

- **Terracotta**: `#c9693a`. Fired clay, used for the ground of Oracle pictures and for figures on Agora cards.
- **Pale Clay**: `#e3a066`. The lighter, reoxidised clay, used for incised lines and highlights.
- **Black Glaze**: `#1b1310`. The sintered slip, used for card bodies, figures and lettering.
- **Added White**: `#efe3cc`. The white-ground panel for rules text; also points medallions and small accents.
- **Added Red**: `#7c2c22`. The purple-red used for manes, hearts and fruit.
- **Ash Grey**: `#5b524b`. The grey of a fire-cloud, used for quiet secondary marks.

## Typography

- **Headers**: Marcellus SC, a Roman-inscriptional capital face that echoes the letters painted on vases.
- **Body text**: Alegreya Sans, a humanist sans that stays readable at small card sizes.

## Best used for

Card faces, card backs and the table of Rivals of Olympus.
